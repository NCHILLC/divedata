# DiveData

DiveData is a free, open-source Android app for diving parents and fans. It lets you follow your diver at a meet: where they are in the dive order, their scores, their place, and what they need to move up. It reads only what DiveMeets already publishes, on your own phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

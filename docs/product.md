# Product

## Who it's for

Parents at a dive meet sit through long sessions waiting for one diver, then find their scores on a
DiveMeets page that is hard to read on a phone. DiveData puts their diver front and centre: where they
are in the order, their scores, their place, and what they need to move up.

Success is diving parents adopting it: it spreads between families and clubs because it is easier
than the website. Parents and fans come first. Divers, coaches and officials may come later, so the
data model must not shut them out.

## Version 1

1. **Find a meet and see results**: by name search, meet number or a pasted DiveMeets link. Events,
   dive order and scores in plain view.
2. **Follow my diver**: save favourite divers; the app opens straight to their event, place in the
   order and scores.
3. **"Up next" alerts**: a notification when a followed diver is close to diving (3 divers away by
   default, changeable in settings).
4. **What-they-need math**: compare two divers round by round, and show the average judge score
   needed to make the cut, place or win.

Not in version 1: iPhone (planned later), Google Play or F-Droid (GitHub download only), tools for
judges and officials, score entry. The app only reads; it never sends anything to DiveMeets.

## Data and network

Storage is on the phone only: followed divers, settings and cached pages. The app talks to DiveMeets
pages, plus one daily check of GitHub Releases for a newer version. Nothing else.

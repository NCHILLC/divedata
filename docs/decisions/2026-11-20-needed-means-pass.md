# "Needed" means finishing above the target

2026-11-20: The score a diver "needs" means finishing above the target. A tie is not a pass. What to assume about rivals' remaining dives is decided in milestone 6.

`averageAwardNeeded` assumes 3 kept awards per dive. That holds for 3, 5 and 7 judges; synchronized panels differ. Revisit in milestone 6.

Why: moving up means passing someone. Saying a tie is enough would tell a parent their diver moved up when they did not.

# Meet search by name filters the meets DiveMeets lists

2026-11-20: DiveMeets has no page that searches meets by name. Its "Find" menu covers divers, teams, pools
and dives, and its "Meets" menu lists meets by year and association (checked in a browser the same day).
So "find a meet by name" filters the meets on the DiveMeets home page (`index.php`: current and upcoming
meets). A past meet opens by its meet number or a pasted DiveMeets link. No new page is loaded for a search.

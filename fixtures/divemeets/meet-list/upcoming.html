What is DiveMeets?

DiveMeets is the most advanced and comprehensive Diving Meet Management System in the world. DiveMeets, via its website and Meet Control software provides a start to finish system for running a Diving Meet.

How Does it work?

  • Meet Director sends meet information/flyer to DiveMeets (at least 3 weeks prior to start of meet)
  • DiveMeets opens registration for the meets on its website
  • Divers visit the DiveMeets website and sign up online for the meet
  • Online signup closes typically 24 hours before the start of the meet
  • Meet Director (and / or support staff) download and install the Meet Control software
  • Using the DiveMeets software, the entries are downloaded and used to prep the meet
  • The Meet Control software is used to run the meet.
